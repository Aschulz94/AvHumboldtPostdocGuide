# AvHumboldtPostdocGuide

A step-by-step guide, tips, and template documents for applying to the **Alexander von Humboldt Research Fellowship** (postdoctoral track, also applicable to the experienced researcher track).

> 📌 Compiled from the [official Humboldt Foundation website](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/humboldt-research-fellowship). This repo collects that information in one place, along with templates and practical advice for putting together a strong application.

---

## About the Fellowship

The Humboldt Research Fellowship is open to researchers of **all nationalities and research areas**, supporting a research stay in Germany. As a fellow, you benefit from individual support from the Humboldt Foundation and its broader sponsorship portfolio.

> Looking ahead to the upcoming selection committee meetings, the Foundation expects that **20–25% of applications** received will be approved.

## Eligibility

Full eligibility details are on the [Humboldt Foundation site](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/humboldt-research-fellowship) under "What is required of you." The primary requirements, adapted from the official page, are:

- **Nationality:** You are not a German national. If you are, or if you completed your university entrance qualification and degree, or degree and doctorate, in Germany, you may still apply if you've lived and worked abroad for more than ten years and intend to continue doing so long-term. If you've lived and worked abroad for more than five years, you may still qualify if there is a strong connection to your current country of residence (permanent employment, additional citizenship, etc. — see the official FAQs).
- **Time in Germany:** You may not have lived in Germany for longer than a total of **six months within the 18-month period** before submitting your application. If you've recently arrived in Germany, submit as soon as possible — eligibility can only be assessed after submission.
- **Host mentor:** A researcher at a German academic institution of your choice must provide a mentoring agreement, written confirmation that research facilities are available, and a detailed review.
- **Expert reviews:**
  - *Postdocs:* ideally your doctoral supervisor plus one other researcher outside your institute.
  - *Experienced researchers:* important collaborative partners and/or researchers at your institute and other institutes, outside your country of origin if possible.
- **Scientific integrity:** You must abide by the rules of good scientific practice and the principles of scientific ethics (see the Foundation's guidance on generative AI in the selection process).
- **Prior sponsorship:** You may not apply if you have already received Humboldt Foundation sponsorship — alumni programs are available instead.

## How to Apply

Applications are submitted **online** via the [Humboldt application portal](https://service.humboldt-foundation.de/pls/web/pub_register.main?p_package=hfst&p_lang=en). Questions before submitting can be sent to **info@avh.de**.

### Required Submission Items

1. Online questionnaire form (research stay location, duration, etc.)
2. Curriculum Vitae — **2 pages max**, publications excluded
3. Research Outline — **5 pages max**, citations and figures included in the page count
4. Complete list of publications ([example](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/fileadmin/Bewerben/Allgemein/sample_list_of_publications.pdf))
5. List of selected key publications ([example](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/fileadmin/Bewerben/Allgemein/sample_list_of_key_publications.pdf))
6. PDF attachments of the selected key publications from item 5
7. Doctoral certificate, proof of completion, or confirmation that it will be completed within 6 months
8. German language certificate, if required by your institute
9. If applicable: publisher's acceptance notifications/acknowledgments and translations of key publications not available in German or English
10. Host's statement and confirmation of research facilities
11. Two expert reviews (letters of recommendation)

Once your online application is received, you'll get written confirmation by email, followed by notice of your expected selection date.

## Deadlines and Timeline

The selection committee meets **three times a year: March, July, and November**.

**Selection procedure overview:**

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Application** | Online application, host's statement, references, receipt of application (acknowledgment sent) |
| **Processing** (~4–6 weeks) | Eligibility check → formal rejection if ineligible; completeness check with notification of expected decision date and reminders for missing items |
| **Review process** (~4 months) | Request for ~2 independent peer reviews (checked for impartiality); reviewed applications forwarded to committee members; selection committee meets (3x/year) with decisions communicated 2–3 working days after the meeting |
| **Selection** (Feb/Mar, June/Jul, Oct/Nov) | Approval → award documents delivered ~4 weeks after the meeting; rejection → reasons available on request ~5 weeks after the meeting; fellowship start (incl. any preceding language course) min. 2, max. 12 months after the meeting |

### Research Statement — Key Hints from the Online Form

- Briefly describe the **current state of research**, supported by ~5 relevant publications (~1 page).
- Clearly describe the **questions you intend to address**, their originality, and their significance for the field (~2 pages).
- Clearly describe and reference the **academic methods** you'll use (~2 pages).
- Total length: **~5 pages including references**. If significantly longer, you may be asked to shorten it.
- You must demonstrate that **you** independently drew up the main content and agreed on it with your host beforehand. Any content contributed by the host institute must be attributed accordingly.

### Publication List — Key Hints from the Online Form

- Draw up a **complete, reverse-chronological** publication list using the official model — non-conforming formats are not accepted.
- **Verify accuracy** (author order/completeness, titles, venues) before uploading — inaccuracies can lead to formal rejection and may violate the Rules of Good Scientific Practice.
- Do **not** include unsubmitted works.
- Only provide title and authors for submitted publications.
- Non-German/English works need the original title plus a German or English translation.
- Mark the corresponding author with an asterisk (per the sample list).
- Indicate at the bottom which publications derive from your dissertation.
- The publication list **cannot be amended** during the selection procedure.

### Key Publications List — Key Hints from the Online Form

- Use the official model format only.
- Postdocs: **1–3** key publications. Experienced researchers: **3–5**.
- Any surplus entries will be removed without notice.
- Same accuracy requirements as the full publication list.
- Only published, accepted, or submitted works qualify (your unpublished dissertation may be a possible exception).
- Your choice of key publications **cannot be amended** during the selection procedure.

## Academic / Professional Record

In addition to the 2-page CV, provide a **seamless, reverse-chronological overview** of every career stage (per `humboldtfellowship_sample_cv.pdf`), including remote work activities.

- List **all periods of interrupted academic career** after your doctorate (partial or complete) — e.g., parental leave, military service, severe illness. These may extend the program's post-doctorate time limit.
- Include: type of university degree/field of study, funding body (if fellowship-supported), % time dedicated to research, reason for any interruption, and (for parental leave) child's date of birth and % of scientific work conducted during that period, if relevant.

## Short Abstract of Research Proposal

Written for a **non-specialist academic audience**, summarizing the main goals and content of your planned research in Germany.

- Avoid unexplained abbreviations.
- State whether laboratory equipment is required.
- **Title:** 200 characters max
- **Keywords:** up to 5 — choose carefully, as they help identify suitable independent peer reviewers
- **Brief summary:** 2,000 characters max
- **Impact statement** — "What do you think will be the impact of your research on the further development of your own academic profile?": 2,000 characters max
