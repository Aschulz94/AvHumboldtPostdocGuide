# AvHumboldtPostdocGuide

A step-by-step guide, downloadable templates, and a growing library of **real, successful application materials** for the **Alexander von Humboldt Research Fellowship** — for postdocs (and experienced researchers) who want to do research in Germany.

[![License: CC BY 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
[![Contributions welcome](https://img.shields.io/badge/contributions-welcome-brightgreen.svg)](CONTRIBUTING.md)

> 📌 Requirements here are compiled from the [official Humboldt Foundation website](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/humboldt-research-fellowship). Rules, character limits, and forms change — **always check the official page before you submit.** The templates in this repo were last updated in November 2023.

---

## Why this repo exists

Applying for a Humboldt is a very different exercise from a U.S.-style fellowship or faculty application: a 2-page CV with no publications, a separate "official model" publication list, a key-publications list with written justifications, and an online form that pulls parts of your proposal into character-limited boxes. Most applicants have never seen a finished one.

Inspired by community collections of [successful job and grant applications](https://www.ogrants.org/), this repo has three parts:

| | What's in it | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 📖 **Guide** | Eligibility, required documents, timeline, and field-by-field hints | This page |
| 📄 **Templates** | Downloadable, editable starting files for every document you write yourself | [`templates/`](templates/) |
| 🗂️ **Example Library** | Successful materials shared by Humboldt fellows across fields | [`examples/`](examples/) |

## About the Fellowship

The Humboldt Research Fellowship is open to researchers of **all nationalities and research areas**, supporting a research stay at a host institution in Germany. As a fellow, you benefit from individual support from the Humboldt Foundation and its broader sponsorship and alumni network.

There are two tracks (definitions from the 2023 online form):

| Track | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| **Postdoctoral researchers** | Completed your doctorate **less than 4 years** ago |
| **Experienced researchers** | Clearly defined academic profile; completed your doctorate **less than 12 years** ago |

> The Foundation expects that **20–25% of applications** will be approved at upcoming selection meetings.

## Eligibility

Full details are on the [Humboldt Foundation site](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/humboldt-research-fellowship) under "What is required of you." The main requirements, adapted from the official page:

- **Nationality:** You are not a German national. If you are, or if you completed your university entrance qualification and degree, or degree and doctorate, in Germany, you may still apply if you've lived and worked abroad for more than ten years and intend to continue doing so long-term. If you've lived and worked abroad for more than five years, you may still qualify if there is a strong connection to your current country of residence (permanent employment, additional citizenship, etc. — see the official FAQs).
- **Time in Germany:** You may not have lived in Germany for longer than a total of **six months within the 18 months** before submitting. If you've recently arrived in Germany, submit as soon as possible — eligibility is only assessed after submission.
- **Host:** A researcher at a German academic institution of your choice must provide a mentoring agreement, written confirmation that research facilities are available, and a detailed review.
- **Expert reviews:**
  - *Postdocs:* ideally your doctoral supervisor plus one other researcher outside your institute.
  - *Experienced researchers:* important collaborative partners and/or researchers at your institute and other institutes, outside your country of origin if possible.
- **Scientific integrity:** You must follow the rules of good scientific practice and the principles of scientific ethics (see the Foundation's guidance on generative AI in the selection process).
- **Prior sponsorship:** You may not apply if you've already received Humboldt Foundation sponsorship — alumni programs are available instead.

> 💡 **Other routes to the same fellowship:** at the time of the 2023 form, researchers from Brazil applied through the *CAPES-Humboldt Research Fellowship*, and researchers from developing and emerging countries whose work contributes to their country's sustainable development were encouraged to apply for the equivalent *Georg Forster Research Fellowship*. Check the Foundation site for the current list.

## How to Apply

Applications are submitted **online** via the [Humboldt application portal](https://service.humboldt-foundation.de/pls/web/pub_register.main?p_package=hfst&p_lang=en). Questions before submitting can go to **info@avh.de**.

### What you submit

| # | Item | Who prepares it | Template / guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Online questionnaire (location, duration of stay, abstract, etc.) | You | [Form walkthrough](templates/HumboldtOnlineResearchForm_Guide.pdf) |
| 2 | Curriculum vitae — **2 pages max**, no publications | You | [CV](#two-page-cv) |
| 3 | Research outline — **~5 pages**, references and figures included | You (agreed with host) | [Research statement](#research-statement-research-outline) |
| 4 | Complete list of publications (official model) | You | [Publication list](#complete-list-of-publications) |
| 5 | List of selected key publications (official model) | You | [Key publications](#list-of-key-publications) |
| 6 | PDFs of the key publications from item 5 | You | — |
| 7 | Doctoral certificate, proof of completion, or confirmation it will be completed within 6 months | You | — |
| 8 | German language certificate, *only if your host institute requires it* | You | — |
| 9 | If applicable: publisher acceptance/receipt notices; summaries/translations of key publications not in German or English | You | — |
| 10 | Host's statement + confirmation of research facilities | **Host** | [Host section](#what-your-host-and-reviewers-submit) |
| 11 | Two expert reviews (letters of recommendation) | **Reviewers** | [Reviewer section](#what-your-host-and-reviewers-submit) |

Once your application is received, you'll get an email confirmation, followed by notice of your expected selection date.

## Deadlines and Timeline

There are **no fixed submission deadlines** — applications are accepted year-round. The selection committee meets **three times a year: March, July, and November**, and your application is assigned to a meeting once it's complete. Plan for **roughly 6–7 months** from a complete application to a decision.

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Application** | You submit online; host's statement and references are uploaded; receipt is acknowledged |
| **Processing** (~4–6 weeks) | Eligibility check (formal rejection if ineligible); completeness check, with notification of expected decision date and reminders for missing items |
| **Review** (~4 months) | ~2 independent peer reviews are requested (checked for impartiality); reviewed applications go to the selection committee; decisions communicated 2–3 working days after the meeting |
| **Selection** (Feb/Mar, Jun/Jul, Oct/Nov) | Approval → award documents ~4 weeks after the meeting. Rejection → reasons available on request ~5 weeks after. Fellowship starts (incl. any language course) **2–12 months** after the meeting |

> ⏱️ **Practical tip:** your application isn't complete until your host and both reviewers have uploaded. Ask them early and give them a firm date.

## Writing Each Document

### Two-Page CV

📄 **Template:** [`templates/2pageCV_Example.pdf`](templates/2pageCV_Example.pdf)

- **Two pages, no publications.** Publications go in their own list (item 4).
- Section order used in the template: **Education → Experience → Awards → Teaching → Invited Talks → Leadership & Service**.
- **Use unambiguous dates.** `03/04/2022` means 4 March to a U.S. reader and 3 April to a German one. Year ranges (`2018–2022`) or written months (`Nov 2023`) avoid the problem entirely.
- CV conventions differ between countries — see the [UChicago CV guide](https://ccrf.uchicago.edu/national-fellowships/building-curriculum-vitae-cv) and this overview of [EU vs. international CV differences](https://www.topcv.com/career-advice/international-cv-differences).

### Research Statement (Research Outline)

📄 **Template:** [`templates/research-statement-latex/`](templates/research-statement-latex/) (or the [`.zip`](templates/HumboldtApp_OverleafTemplate.zip) for Overleaf). Word is also fine.

The LaTeX template is organized so you can draft **everything in one place**:

```
Title page  ──►  copy/paste into the ONLINE FORM (not part of the PDF)
  ├─ Title
  ├─ Summary        (≤ 2,000 characters incl. spaces)
  └─ Impact         (≤ 2,000 characters incl. spaces)

Pages 1–5  ──►  exported as the RESEARCH OUTLINE PDF (references count toward the 5 pages)
  ├─ Introduction
  ├─ Project Significance
  ├─ What Questions Are to Be Examined?   (+ brief timeline)
  ├─ Proposed Project Methodology          (organized by research question)
  └─ References
```

The question/methodology structure is one approach that worked — it is not required by the Foundation.

**What the online form asks for:**

- Briefly describe the **current state of research**, supported by ~5 relevant publications (~1 page).
- Clearly describe the **questions you intend to address**, their originality, and their significance for the field (~2 pages).
- Clearly describe and reference the **methods** you'll use (~2 pages).
- **~5 pages total, including references.** Significantly longer outlines may be sent back for shortening.
- You must show that **you** independently drew up the main content and agreed on it with your host beforehand. Any content contributed by the host institute must be attributed accordingly.

### Complete List of Publications

📄 **Template:** [`templates/Humboldt_ListOfPubs_Example.docx`](templates/Humboldt_ListOfPubs_Example.docx) · [official sample](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/fileadmin/Bewerben/Allgemein/sample_list_of_publications.pdf)

- Use the **official model format** — non-conforming formats are not accepted.
- **Complete and reverse-chronological**, split into sections (peer-reviewed journals, submitted, conference, book chapters…).
- **Verify accuracy** (author order and completeness, titles, venues). Inaccuracies can lead to formal rejection and may violate the Rules of Good Scientific Practice.
- Do **not** include unsubmitted work. For submitted work, give only title and authors.
- Mark the **corresponding author with an asterisk (\*)**.
- Indicate at the bottom which publications **derive from your dissertation**.
- Non-German/English works need the original title plus a German or English translation.
- ⚠️ The list **cannot be amended** during the selection procedure.

### List of Key Publications

📄 **Template:** [`templates/ListOfKeyPublications_Example.docx`](templates/ListOfKeyPublications_Example.docx) · [official sample](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/fileadmin/Bewerben/Allgemein/sample_list_of_key_publications.pdf)

- **Postdocs: 1–3** key publications. **Experienced researchers: 3–5.** Surplus entries are removed without notice.
- Each entry = **full citation + a short paragraph** explaining what the paper showed, *your* contribution, and how it connects to the project you're proposing in Germany. This paragraph is where you build the case — don't skip it.
- Only published, accepted, or submitted work qualifies (your unpublished dissertation may be an exception).
- Same accuracy rules as the full list; ⚠️ your choice **cannot be amended** later.

### Online Form: Abstract, Keywords & Impact

📄 **Walkthrough:** [`templates/HumboldtOnlineResearchForm_Guide.pdf`](templates/HumboldtOnlineResearchForm_Guide.pdf)

Written for a **non-specialist academic audience**. Avoid unexplained abbreviations, and state whether laboratory equipment is required.

| Field | Limit |
|---|---|
| Title | 200 characters |
| Keywords | up to 5 — they're used to find your peer reviewers, so choose carefully |
| Brief summary | 2,000 characters (spaces counted in 2023) |
| Impact statement — *"What do you think will be the impact of your research on the further development of your own academic profile?"* | 2,000 characters |

Citations can't be formatted in these boxes, so write them to stand on their own.

### Academic / Professional Record

In addition to the 2-page CV, the online form asks for a **seamless, reverse-chronological overview** of every career stage, including remote work.

- List **all career interruptions** after your doctorate (partial or complete) — e.g., parental leave, military service, severe illness. These can **extend the program's post-doctorate time limit.**
- For each entry: degree type/field, funding body (if fellowship-supported), % of time dedicated to research, reason for any interruption, and — for parental leave — the child's date of birth and the % of scientific work done during that period, if relevant.

### Field of Research

Choose a research area from the Foundation's **Research Area Index**; you may add a second area.

## What Your Host and Reviewers Submit

**Host.** You send your host an upload link that also contains information for hosts, the list of questions for the host's statement, and the *Confirmation of Research Facilities* form. Make sure your host uses the current version from that link.

**Expert reviewers.** Each reviewer receives an upload link with information for reviewers and the list of questions for expert reviews. Share your research outline and CV with them so their letters can speak to the specific project.

## Pre-Submission Checklist

- [ ] Host has agreed on the project and is ready to upload their statement + facilities form
- [ ] Both expert reviewers have been asked and given a date
- [ ] CV is **2 pages**, no publications, dates unambiguous
- [ ] Research outline is **~5 pages including references**, and host contributions are attributed
- [ ] Summary and impact statement are each **≤ 2,000 characters** (spaces included) and readable by a non-specialist
- [ ] Up to **5 keywords**, chosen to attract the right reviewers
- [ ] Full publication list: official model, reverse-chronological, corresponding authors marked (\*), dissertation-derived papers marked, "as of" date
- [ ] Key publications: correct number for your track, each with a justification paragraph, PDFs attached
- [ ] Author lists triple-checked — **neither list can be amended later**
- [ ] Doctoral certificate (or confirmation) ready; language certificate only if your host requires it
- [ ] Every career interruption listed in the academic record

## Contributing Your Materials

We'd love to include your successful Humboldt materials — any field, any year, either track. Full instructions are in [**CONTRIBUTING.md**](CONTRIBUTING.md). In short:

1. **Email** [aschulz@is.mpg.de](mailto:aschulz@is.mpg.de) your files, **or** open a pull request adding them to the right `examples/` folder.
2. Send **PDFs**, named `<LastName>_<DocType>_<Year>_<Field>.pdf` (e.g., `Schulz_ResearchStatement_2023_Biomechanics.pdf`).
3. Redact anything you don't want public (phone numbers, addresses, unpublished results).
